Account and progress
You can use public features without an account. When you register, Firebase Authentication handles your email and password. Your name and avatar are stored in your private Firebase profile. Quran reading positions sync when you choose Sync Quran progress. Prayer history syncs when you choose Sync prayer history. These records belong to your signed-in account.

On this device
Reading positions, bookmarks, habits, prayer records and preferences are stored on your device. Zakat amounts and calculations stay on this device; only the selected currency is sent to quote providers. Saved location is used for prayer times and reminders. Location permission can be changed in Android Settings. Notifications are optional. Android backup of Seeker data is disabled in this release. Clearing app storage removes device-only progress.

External services
Firebase provides account and cloud storage services. Mosque Finder opens Google Maps; your chosen search and saved coordinates, when available, are passed to Google Maps. Seeker does not send Places API requests. Metal reference prices are requested from Snapdata and goldprice.dev, which receive normal connection data such as IP addresses. Embedded YouTube videos and TV-provider pages connect to their providers and may use cookies, logs or advertising according to their policies. Seeker does not control those services. Quran and Hadith text bundled with the app can be read offline.

Delete your account
In Profile, choose Delete account and confirm your password. This deletes your Firebase sign-in account and its Seeker cloud profile, prayer records and reading positions. Account-specific prayer records on this device are removed. Shared device reading positions and other device-only preferences remain until you clear app storage. A minimal deletion marker is retained to block delayed writes from old login tokens. Security or operational logs may remain under the service providers' retention policies.

You can also request account deletion from huzaifmushtaq775@gmail.com using your registered email. We must verify ownership before deleting an account. Never send your password. Contact the same address for privacy questions or corrections.
