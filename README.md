# Seeker – Koshur Islamic Guide

**Your Companion for Quran, Hadith, Duas & Islamic Learning**

Seeker is a free, ad-free Islamic application designed to make authentic Islamic knowledge, Quranic learning, and essential religious resources accessible to everyone, with special support for Kashmiri-speaking Muslims.

Whether you want to read the Holy Quran, understand its meaning in Kashmiri, explore Hadith, learn through Quran classes, or stay connected with your daily prayers, Seeker brings everything together in one place.

### Key Features

** Holy Quran**
- Read the Holy Quran with translations and Tafsir.
- Explore Kashmiri Quran translations to understand the meaning of Allah's words.
- Enjoy a convenient and accessible Quran reading experience.

** Hadith & Islamic Knowledge**
- Explore Hadith collections and Islamic teachings.
- Learn about Islamic principles, guidance, and everyday religious practices.

** Duas & Supplications**
- Access Islamic Duas and supplications.
- Find prayers for different occasions and daily activities.

** Prayer Times & Qibla Direction**
- Stay informed about daily Salah timings.
- Find the Qibla direction to perform your prayers.

** Islamic Learning & Quran Classes**
- Access Quran learning opportunities through Zoom classes.
- Explore Kashmiri-language Quran classes.
- Strengthen your understanding of Islam through educational resources.

### Made for Kashmir, Accessible to Everyone

Seeker places special emphasis on making Islamic knowledge accessible to Kashmiri-speaking communities while welcoming Muslims from every region and background.

Our aim is to bridge language barriers and make learning Islam easier, more meaningful, and accessible.

### Completely Free. No Ads. No Commercial Intentions.

Seeker is developed as a humble effort to serve the Muslim community and contribute something beneficial to humanity.

- Free to download and use.
- No advertisements.
- No commercial promotions.
- Built with the intention of spreading beneficial knowledge.

### Our Mission

Knowledge should be accessible to everyone, regardless of language, location, or financial circumstances.

Seeker is built upon a simple belief: **every person deserves an opportunity to seek knowledge, understand their faith, and grow closer to Allah (SWT).**

May Allah accept this humble effort and make it a source of Sadaqah Jariyah.

**Seeker — Seek Knowledge. Strengthen Faith.**
