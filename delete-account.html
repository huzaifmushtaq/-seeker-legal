Delete your Seeker account

Publisher: Huzaif Mushtaq

In Seeker, open Profile → Delete account and confirm your password. This permanently removes your Firebase sign-in identity and Seeker cloud profile, prayer records and Quran reading positions.

Request deletion without the app

Email huzaifmushtaq775@gmail.com from your registered email address. State that you want your Seeker account and cloud data deleted. Ownership must be verified before deletion. Never send your password.

Shared device reading progress and preferences remain until you clear app storage. Account-specific prayer data on the device used for in-app deletion is removed. Security and operational logs may remain under the providers' retention policies.
